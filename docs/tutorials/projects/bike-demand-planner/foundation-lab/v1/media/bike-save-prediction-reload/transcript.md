## Preserve changed inputs and existing limitations

Change the requested hour to two in the morning and the forecast becomes seven point five one. Change to a Sunday at eight and it becomes seventy six point four three. A June Monday at eight still receives three hundred and forty nine point five zero, because the function uses only hour and working day status. Perfect reloading preserves this lack of month sensitivity. It does not improve quality. All forty eight real groups are present; a separate small fixture establishes the absent key fallback. Invalid inputs instead fail before lookup. Those behaviors belong to different cases.
