# What the completed project establishes

## The local application is implemented

The saved prediction contract, frozen data preparation, model comparisons, error review, inference bundle, complete local training recovery and later assessment are implemented and checked. The [completion record](project/implementation.md) and the [source README](evidence/project-readme.txt) describe the runnable boundary.

## Questions to explain in your own words

- Why is the target-hour count unavailable when the forecast is issued?
- Why does the target change from 45 to 35 reverse all three parameter updates?
- Why can a group baseline outperform an additive linear model and a larger forest in validation?
- Why do missing hours remain absent from metric denominators?
- Which state is required to reproduce the next Adam update, beyond the weights?
- What changes in the claim if the forest is selected after observing its final-test advantage?

The [worked review questions](practice/explain-your-model.md) are available when useful. They are optional review, not a typing exercise or evidence of a learner answer.
