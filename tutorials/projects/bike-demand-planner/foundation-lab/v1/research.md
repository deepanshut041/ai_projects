# Sources, retained choices and evidence limits

The tutorial consolidates the completed Bike Demand Planner implementation at `0b49c0a`. Its primary evidence is the inspected Python source, saved numerical examples and historical experiment receipts linked beside each worked calculation. The detailed explanations are retained from the original understanding notes; this is a change of teaching location and navigation, not a new experiment.

## Dataset and forecasting question

The source is Fanaee-T’s UCI Bike Sharing dataset (2013), DOI 10.24432/C5W894, licensed CC BY 4.0. The project uses the official hourly archive. Attribution, source/archive identities and acquisition details are retained in lesson 2 and in the project’s generated source receipt. The dataset’s naive timestamps do not establish a time zone or daylight-saving interpretation. The declared forecast uses calendar information available at issue time, one hour before the target interval starts. Same-hour observed weather and rental components remain excluded.

## Original records and media

The [migration map](evidence/migration.json) records each original notes page and its unchanged digest. Original records remain ignored in the project, preserving the exact source files pinned by existing video production receipts. The tutorial’s new paths, headings and reference links do not rewrite those old production claims. Forty-seven existing short companions and four newly authored companions for the opening prediction/update lesson are embedded beside their matching explanations. The [local movie manifest](local-videos.json) binds the selected finished MP4 bytes; captions, posters and transcripts are frozen in this package. The initial conversion reused the existing media. The four opening-lesson companions were then narrated separately to fill the missing section videos; the model implementation and its historical experiments are unchanged. The earlier long overview film remains an archived alternative rather than another required start.

## Method and proof boundaries

The first update is explicitly illustrative arithmetic. The later hourly examples, fitted values, comparison results and recovery behavior are observations from the retained project. The final protocol precedes target exposure and preserves the validation-selected candidate. Reconstructing those historical results does not provide another independent generalization sample. No test count, source inspection or tutorial availability establishes learner mastery, hosted recovery or an externally deployed system.

The [reference results](project/reference-runs.md), [current completion record](project/implementation.md) and [readiness questions](project/readiness.md) preserve those distinctions.
