The forest's later advantage is a real result, while its earlier validation disadvantage remains real too. Retaining the original selection keeps that development history inspectable. It does not force an operational deployment of the weaker later model. A later forest choice should be recorded as informed by this test, and a new independent quality claim should use new held-out evidence.

The group model is also smaller: 5,640 JSON bytes versus the forest's 16,087,600. Historical fit and final saved-batch timings are retained with their scopes. They do not measure peak memory, optimized request latency or downstream planning value. These costs can inform a later decision, but they do not override the frozen evaluation definition.

Changing only working-day status through the recorded 4 July holiday changes the group forecast to 76.4348. Changing month while retaining the working-day hour-8 key does not change it. These verified examples reveal the fitted function's structure. They do not identify why the observed demand level changes or establish calibrated uncertainty.
